## 1. Shape of the lesson

Nine chapters, 52 shots, **6 min 21 s** of film plus learner-paced tasks and the recall challenge.

| # | Chapter | Shots | Length | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Two joints, two movements (Hook) | 4 | 25.4 s | shoulder vs elbow, then the question: why do they move so differently? |
| 02 | What is a joint? | 3 | 23.6 s | bones meet; the shape of the meeting decides the movement |
| 03 | Fixed joint | 6 | 45.8 s | arrive → look → movement → **why** → name → **your task** |
| 04 | Pivot joint | 6 | 43.2 s | the same six beats |
| 05 | Ball-and-socket joint | 6 | 44.8 s | the same six beats |
| 06 | Hinge joint | 13 | 116.2 s | the source sentence, bones, axis, flexion, extension, **why**, name, your turn, the 90° check, capsule and ligaments, the knee |
| 07 | Comparing the four | 6 | 40.7 s | one pattern at a time, then all four |
| 08 | Recall challenge | 2 + learner-paced | 11.6 s | five questions: see the movement, name the category |
| 09 | The body map | 6 | 30.0 s | the memory anchor |

The four category chapters share one declared shape. Each gives a **reason** for its movement, and each ends in a
**learner task** inside its exploration: fixed, pivot and ball-and-socket hand over automatically, and the elbow
through its in-film "your turn" and 90° check.

## 2. What the learner reads and hears

The heading is shown at the top left under the chapter line; the spoken sentence is the narration, also shown as a
subtitle at the bottom.

| Chapter | Headline (on screen) → spoken |
|---|---|
| **01** | "Your shoulder can swing your arm in almost any direction." → "Start with the shoulder. Your arm can swing forward, out to the side, and round in a circle." · "Your elbow mainly bends and straightens." → "…It does one thing: it bends and it straightens." · "Both are joints. So why do they move so differently?" |
| **02** | "A joint is a place where bones meet." → "A joint is any place where bones meet. Most joints are two bones meeting; here at the elbow, three bones come together." · "The way the bones meet decides how they can move." · "…We will look at four kinds, and at what each shape allows." |
| **03 Fixed** | "Several bones, one skull" → "Two bones, one join" → "Almost no movement" → **"Why they cannot move"**: "The edges of these bones lock into each other, and tough fibres hold them tightly together…" → name → "Your turn" |
| **04 Pivot** | "The top of the neck" → "One bone on top of another" (nothing moving yet) → "The upper bone turns" → **"Turning, and nothing else"**: "…turns around one line running straight up through both bones…" → name → "Your turn" |
| **05 Ball-and-socket** | "Where the arm meets the body" → "A round end and a hollow" → "Movement in many directions" → **"Why it moves so freely"**: "A rounded end sitting in a hollow can roll and turn in every direction at once…" → name → "Your turn" |
| **06 Hinge** | the source sentence → "Three bones meet here" → the axis (a blue line) → flexion → extension → **"Why it bends only one way"**: "The end of the upper-arm bone is like a spool, and the forearm bone wraps around it…" → "Hinge joint" → "Your turn: bend and straighten the elbow." → "Try it: bend the elbow to about 90°." → capsule and ligaments → the knee |
| **07 Compare** | "Four joints. Four different ways of moving." → "The skull: edges locked together, so almost no movement." · "The top of the neck: one bone turning around another." · "The shoulder: a round end in a hollow, so movement in many directions." · "The elbow: bending and straightening around one line." → "The shape of the join decides what the joint can do." |
| **08 Recall** | "Your turn. Name each joint from the way it moves." → five questions, each naming only a movement |
| **09 Body map** | each site with its category and one sentence → "Four joins, four shapes, four kinds of movement. The shape of the join is what decides." |

Vocabulary is withheld until movement has been seen: chapters 01 and 02 never say fixed, pivot, ball-and-socket or
hinge, and a test asserts it.

- [ ] **T4** The hook creates a genuine question before any category is named. — **PENDING**
