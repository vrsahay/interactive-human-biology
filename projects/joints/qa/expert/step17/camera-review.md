## 1. How the film is shot

| Chapter | Framing |
|---|---|
| 01 Hook | whole body → the right shoulder → the right elbow → whole body |
| 02 What is a joint? | the elbow close up → the whole body |
| 03 Fixed | 6 shots on the skull suture, the joint site framed right of centre |
| 04 Pivot | 6 shots on the top of the neck, including one from behind and level so the two bones are visible (from the front the jaw hides them) |
| 05 Ball-and-socket | 6 shots on the right shoulder |
| 06 Hinge | the elbow site; focus and close-ups on the bones and capsule; four **movement** shots looking along the flexion axis, so bending is seen in its plane; then the knee |
| 07 Compare | whole body → each of the four sites in turn → whole body |
| 08 Recall | whole body, with each question lighting its joint |
| 09 Body map | whole body |

- 50 of 52 shots travel into their framing; the other 2 cut.
- Under reduced motion every shot cuts, and nothing dollies during a shot.
- The subject is composed to one side on desktop so the topic heading (top left) and the anatomy do not compete.

Framings are sized from each joint site's framing sphere, which the delivery manifest's close-up spheres cap. A test
asserts that every close shot stays inside them.
