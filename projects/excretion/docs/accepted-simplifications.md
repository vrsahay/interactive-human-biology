# Accepted simplifications: do not fix

These are deliberate Class 10 simplifications, checked in the deep audit (`deep-biological-audit.md`, 2026-09-22). Each one is either true to NCERT or goes beyond NCERT without contradicting it (**SBK** = supplementary biological knowledge), and none leads a learner to a wrong conclusion about what the lesson teaches. **Do not "correct" these in V2.4.**

Classes:
- **A**: symbolic glyph
- **B**: reduced structure
- **C**: omitted detail
- **D**: exaggerated scale or timing

| # | Simplification | Where | Class | Why it stays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Molecules and water are drawn as glyphs: O₂ = pale-blue pair, CO₂ = linear O=C=O with a dark centre, water = blue drop, vapour = white puff, waste = purple crystal, resin = amber mass, soil waste = purple bead | all particle streams, `17_waste_visuals` | A | Distinct shapes and colours are what make HEAR = SEE possible; true molecular scale would make them invisible. |
| 2 | Particle sizes, numbers and speeds are exaggerated | all processes | D | Needed for visibility; no quantity is taught. |
| 3 | The guard cells visibly shrink and thin when the pore closes | `03_stoma` `Open` morph | D | Matches NCERT's word "shrink" (p83). SBK: real guard cells change shape more than volume. |
| 4 | Stomata appear only on the lower epidermis | `05_leaf_internal`, `03_stoma` | C | The common dicot pattern; NCERT does not specify. |
| 5 | Night CO₂ leaves through a (thinned) stoma stream | `night.night` | B | NCERT gives no route. SBK: most stomata are nearly closed at night, which the thin, slow stream reflects (C-11, S6). Not narrated. |
| 6 | O₂ forms at the disc stacks; CO₂ is used in the stroma; no biochemical steps | `08_chloroplast` | B | SBK-correct placement; NCERT p81 gives only inputs and outputs. |
| 7 | About 13 grana with lamellae; no ribosomes, starch grains or DNA | `08_chloroplast` | C | Only "discs with chlorophyll" (C9 p18) is taught. |
| 8 | The plant cell is a cut-away box: vacuole about 55% of the volume, chloroplasts along the walls, gaps between neighbouring cells | `06_plant_cell` | B | C9 p19 (large vacuole); the gaps are the leaf's intercellular air spaces (C10 p88). |
| 9 | The leaf vein is a simple bundle: a few xylem rings above a small group of phloem, inside a bundle sheath | `05_leaf_internal` | B | V2.4 correction to this list: the vein does show phloem (`Vein_Phloem`). Only the xylem's role is narrated here. |
| 10 | The epidermis is shown without chloroplasts; the cuticle is a thin translucent skin | `05_leaf_internal` | B | SBK-correct; the cuticle is C9 p32. |
| 11 | Bark and phloem are drawn as separate rings; the cambium is an unlabelled thin green line; the pith is unlabelled | `10_stem_cross_section` | B/C | NCERT does not define bark. SBK: secondary phloem is the inner bark. Naming only bark, phloem, xylem and old xylem keeps the load down. |
| 12 | Growth rings in the xylem | `10_stem_cross_section` | C | Context only; not narrated. |
| 13 | Resin and gum are shown as amber masses filling old vessels | `11_xylem`, `10_stem_cross_section` | A/D | NCERT p98 "resins and gums, especially in old xylem". SBK: heartwood deposits fill the lumens. |
| 14 | Xylem vessels show ring or spiral thickenings only (no pits); tracheids are tapered spindles; fibres are plain tubes; one row of living ray cells | `11_xylem` | B | Supports "tube-like cells with thick walls … a few are living" (C9 p33). |
| 15 | Waste crystals pass through a living ray cell on their way into old vessels | `resin.store` | B | SBK-plausible (resin is made by living parenchyma); not narrated. |
| 16 | The root shows a single central xylem strand (not the star-shaped radial xylem of a dicot root) | `14_root` | B | Only "into its tubes called xylem" is taught; the radial pattern is beyond Class 10. |
| 17 | The root-hair nucleus is drawn in the cell body, not in the hair | `15_root_hairs` | C | Not taught; a visible nucleus marks the hair as part of one living cell. |
| 18 | Soil grains are one size class, with water films shown as halos | `15_root_hairs`, `16_soil` | B | Supports "water in the soil" and "large surface". |
| 19 | Soil waste leaves one surface cell and drifts sideways | `soil.excrete` | D | Matches NCERT "excrete some waste substances into the soil" (p98); S7. |
| 20 | The fallen leaf carries its wastes to the soil in one shot | `leaves.fall` | D | NCERT p98; time-lapse compression. |
| 21 | Cells are coloured by role (green = photosynthetic, cream = wall or non-green) | cell, leaf interior, root | A | Readability; the colour code is consistent across the lesson. |
| 22 | Lighting is camera-attached, and a dark night is signalled by dimming and a blue tint | runtime | A | Presentation only. |
